FFT SPRITE PORTER - MOD MAKER
Turn your FFT sprite into a mod for The Ivalice Chronicles in one minute.


1. Run FFTSpritePorter.exe.

2. Drag your sprite onto the window.
   It takes the same files you already make: the 256x488 .bmp sheet or the .spr.

3. Tick the character or job that should get it.

4. Type the mod name, your name and the sprite artist.

5. Optional: tick "Smooth upscale" to round the jagged edges (see below).

6. Click "Create mod (.zip)".

That zip is the finished mod. Upload it to Nexus as it is.
Players drag it into Reloaded-II, enable it and play. They need nothing else from you.


ABOUT "SMOOTH UPSCALE"

The game's sheets are twice the size of the classic ones, so every pixel of your sprite becomes a 2x2 block.
Smooth upscale is an automatic filter (Scale2x) that rounds the stair-step edges while doing that. It only reuses
the colors already in your sprite, so the palette stays exactly yours.

It is a shortcut, nothing more. A sprite upscaled by hand by a spriter is in another league: the filter cannot add
detail, it only softens edges. Leave it off if you want every pixel exactly as you drew it.


THREE THINGS TO KNOW

- Use the original file. If the .bmp was re-saved as true color or converted from a .png, the colors come out wrong.
- The sprite keeps its own palette. Extra palettes in the file become the alternate team colors.
- The sheet type has to match the character: a TYPE1 sprite on a TYPE1 character, TYPE2 on TYPE2.
  If arms or heads look out of place in the game, it is the other type.


If the tool does not open, install the .NET 9 Desktop Runtime (x64) from Microsoft. Anyone who uses Reloaded-II already has it.

Portraits are not changed. Monsters and Lucavi are not supported.
